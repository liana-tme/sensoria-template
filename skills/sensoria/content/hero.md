# Hero — what the owner changes

File: `src/sections/hero/content.ts`.

- **Photo:** a vivid, colourful beauty portrait or close-up with soft motion blur, on a dark background; landscape, about 2400px wide. **Keep the face in the centre:** on phones only a narrow vertical strip from the middle is visible. On desktop the photo flows under the cursor, so any photo works. Write an alt text that describes the photo.
- **Lead:** one paragraph of two or three short sentences (about 100 characters), like "No more skin guessing. No more random products. …".
- **Subtitle:** a short line, 2–5 words, describing the category or the promise ("Intelligent neuro-beauty"); it stays on one line at every width.
- **Title:** the product name, one word if possible — it is shown in very large capitals.
- **Mark:** two tiny texts beside the ruler on the right edge (a year and a short number).
