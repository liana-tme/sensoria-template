# Get Started — what the owner changes

File: `src/sections/get-started/content.ts`.

- **Strip:** 6–10 short words or phrases in capitals (1–3 words each).
- **Heading:** two parts, about 30 characters together; the second part is softer.
- **Video:** a vertical clip (9:16) in `public/videos/`, plus a poster photo in `public/images/get-started/`. Without the video file, the poster stays.
- **Intro:** one sentence (about 120 characters); mark the key part `bright`.
- **Form:** put the address from a form service (Formspree, Basin, Getform…) in `action`. Until then, `fallbackEmail` receives the messages through the visitor's e-mail app. Labels, placeholders, button texts and the note with its link (privacy page) can be changed.
