# Analysis — what the owner changes

File: `src/sections/analysis/content.ts`.

- **Tag and heading:** `[Analysis]` style label; the heading is one strong word.
- **Video:** the owner's own short product video (MP4, under ~10 MB, works without sound) in `public/videos/`, and a cover photo (landscape, about 2400 px wide) in `public/images/analysis/`. Never use a video or photo from the internet. Until there is a video, leave `src` empty or the file missing: the card shows the cover only.
- **Stats:** three real numbers with a short suffix (`%`, `h`, `s`, `+`) and a 2-word label. `from` is where the count starts (a little below `to`). The template's numbers are examples — replace them or remove the claim.
- **Text:** one or two sentences about how the product works; mark 1–2 short parts `bright`.
