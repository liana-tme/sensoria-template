# Footer — what the owner changes

File: `src/sections/footer/content.ts`.

- **Text:** one sentence (about 130 characters). Keep the spaces at the start: they make the indent.
- **Contact:** label and the e-mail. **Photo:** any calm wide photo; the veil darkens it.
- **Newsletter:** the line, placeholder and the service address in `action` (empty = the visitor's e-mail app writes to `fallbackEmail`).
- **Menu columns:** up to 2 columns, 3–5 links each. The "Social" links are the template author's: replace them with your own profiles.
- **Bottom bar:** copyright year and name, credit line, legal links.
