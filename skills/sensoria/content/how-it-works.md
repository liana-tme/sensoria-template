# How it Works — what the owner changes

File: `src/sections/how-it-works/content.ts`.

- **Tag and heading:** `[How it Works]` style label; heading in two short parts (the second is softer).

- **Steps:** exactly four, numbered 01–04: a 1–2 word title and one sentence (about 70 characters) each. Real steps of the owner's product, no invented numbers.
- **Button:** one short action ("Try …") and where it leads.
