# Pricing — what the owner changes

File: `src/sections/pricing/content.ts`.

- **Plans:** two plans. Real prices for both periods (the yearly one is usually ~20% less than 12 months; update the switch label if not). One-sentence description, up to five short features (about 40 characters each).
- **Buttons:** the link to checkout or sign-up for each plan. **Until then they are placeholders that go nowhere** — tell the owner, and check it before launch. Never invent a payment link.
- **Custom card:** a short tag, a two-part question/answer title, the sales e-mail, a photo (shown dimmed).
- **Founder:** a real quote from the founder (with permission), name, role, square avatar. Never invent a person.
- All template prices, plans and the founder are examples.
