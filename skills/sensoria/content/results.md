# Results — what the owner changes

File: `src/sections/results/content.ts`.

- **Tag and heading:** `[Results]` style label; heading = one strong word + a short accent part in brackets.
- **Highlight card:** a 2-word title, one short quote (about 70 characters), a button label and link. The template quote ("…60% increase in leads") is a leftover sample from the original — replace it.
- **Review:** a real customer's first name and surname (with permission), a short role ("Verified User"), a square avatar photo, two sentences (the first is shown bright), and one measurable line ("Vitality index: 82% → 96%"). Never invent reviews or numbers.
- **Before / after:** two photos of the same person or product from the same angle, landscape. Write what each shows. Only real, permitted photos.
