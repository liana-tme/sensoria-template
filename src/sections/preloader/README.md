# Preloader

The curtain that covers the Home page while it opens. Home only.

## What it does
A dark curtain with a fine grain. Your brand name rises letter by letter in the centre, then the curtain slides up and the hero appears.

## What you can change (in `content.ts`)
| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| `word` | the word in the centre (usually the brand name) |

## Do not change
Timing, colours and motion: the hero entrance is timed to this curtain.
