## 1. Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

- Personal information you provide directly such as your name and email address.
- High-resolution biometric skin data generated through AI neural mapping and scans.
- Usage data, device diagnostic information, and internal analytics data.
- Communications sent to the lab including technical support requests and feedback.

## 2. How we use your information

Your information is used to:

- Provide and improve personalized neural skin diagnostics.
- Generate adaptive and actionable health recommendations based on clinical data.
- Operate, maintain, and optimize Sensoria’s core diagnostic engine.
- Communicate technical updates, research results, and important laboratory notices.
- Ensure the security, performance, and legal compliance of our systems.

## 3. AI & neural mapping data

Our processing standards:

- Skin scans and biometric insights are processed specifically to deliver personalized diagnostic results.
- We do not sell or share identifiable biometric data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 4. Data sharing

We may share data only with:

- Trusted service providers including hosting, secure data storage, and infrastructure partners.
- Legal authorities when strictly required by applicable law or regulation.
- All partners are legally required to protect your data and utilize it only for specified laboratory purposes.

## 5. Data security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures and encryption to protect your biometric information. However, no digital system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmission.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access, update, or permanently delete your personal and biometric data.
- Withdraw consent for neural processing at any time.
- Request data portability or restriction of specific data processing activities.

## 7. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your biometric data, contact us at: contact@sensoria.com.
