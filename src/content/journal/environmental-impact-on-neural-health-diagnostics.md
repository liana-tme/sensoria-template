## 1. The 10% humidity threshold

Our clinical trials show that a drop in ambient humidity below **35%** triggers an immediate inflammatory response in sensitive skin types.

- **The methodology:** We tracked 1,200 participants across four distinct climate zones.
- **The finding:** Skin barrier integrity decreases by an average of **12%** for every 10% drop in relative humidity if the routine is not adjusted within 24 hours.

## 2. UV-induced oxidative stress metrics

Sensoria’s neural engine integrates regional UV Index data to predict oxidative stress before physical symptoms appear.

- **Adaptive SPF Logic:** The system recalibrates recommendation strength based on cumulative exposure forecasts.
- **Recovery protocols:** High-intensity UV exposure triggers an automated shift toward antioxidant-heavy serum recommendations in the user's "Evening Routine".

## 3. Clinical outcome data

Users utilizing our **Climate-Adaptive Mode** showed a **32% improvement** in skin hydration levels compared to the control group using standard dermatological prescriptions over a 90-day period.
